[hr][/hr]
Adds a fully customizable glass shader onto every building, mirroring the city around it and a live sky with SSR (works with the clouds in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3805863507]Volumetric Weather[/url]).
[h3]Water shader[/h3]
This mod also adds reflections (SSR) and foam into the water. To turn it off: press F8 -> Water -> "Realistic water".
[h3]Parallax interiors[/h3]
Adds rooms behind the windows at night (optionally by day too), with lights that turn on and off with the time of day. To turn them off: press F8 -> Interiors -> "Parallax interiors".

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and this mod, and enable both (in Skyve too, if you use it).
[*][b]Load a savegame[/b].
[*][b]Press F8[/b] (or click the mod's button) to change settings.
[*][b]Press Alt+G[/b] and select a building to customize individual buildings.
[/olist]
[h3]Default Settings[/h3]
The default settings were tested on a vanilla game with no LUT, so they might look a bit rough or aggressive if you use an LUT. Feel free to customize it to your liking, you have full control over every setting.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Works [i]well[/i] with most vanilla and custom building assets. No conflicts found with Render It!, AD Cloud Replacer, Move It!, TM:PE, FPS Booster and more. Buildings converted with Procedural Objects get the glass too. Safe to remove: it saves nothing in your cities.
[h3]Known issues / limitations[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]At night, parallax interiors are not lining up with the windows on some buildings[/b]: not all buildings have proper textures that allow the mod to add the glass and interiors properly. Alt+G, select building, then turn off "Parallax interiors" to disable interiors for this building.
[*][b]Glass/Water flickering[/b]: this happens due to using TAA. If it's too distracting, try changing your anti-aliasing method.
[*][b]Reflections are not accurate:[/b] this uses SSR which is approximate, ray tracing is not supported by the game to provide accurate reflections.
[*][b]Some buildings are showing glass in the wrong places:[/b] unfortunately this is not within my control; some buildings don't have proper specular textures. Since 1.1, a building whose distant model (LOD) has no proper specular texture takes its full model's glass instead. Press Alt+G then select the building, and turn down Strength to turn off the shader for this particular building, or change "Glass on its LOD".
[*][b]Glass briefly disappearing on Mac[/b] (and potentially Linux): I'm not sure if this is my slow Mac that's causing this, but if you run into this issue, let me know.
[/list]

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]RealGlass.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Real Glass & Water) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
