[h1]Version 1.0: First Release[/h1]
Makes the glass on your buildings (especially skyscrapers) look real and mirroring the sky, the streets and the towers around it.
Just as with the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3805863507]Volumetric Weather[/url] mod, this mod was tested only on my Windows 11 PC and MacBook. If anything looks wrong or runs badly for you, please let me know in the comments.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to this mod and to Harmony, then make sure both are enabled in [i]Content Manager → Mods[/i]. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the glass is there. It works in the Asset Editor and the Scenario Editor too.
[*][b]Press F8[/b] (or click the mod's button in UUI) to open the in-game panel. Every setting of the glass is there, on four tabs.
[/olist]
The mod's own options (the panel's key, the button) are in the Pause Menu, under [i]Options → Real Glass[/i].
The default settings are tested and work well with LUTs and [b]Render It![/b]. If you've changed things and want to start over, [i]Reset all settings to defaults[/i] is on the same page.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Real glass on every building[/b], near and far. It is read from each asset's own textures, so it works with any building
[*][b]Ten kinds of real glass[/b], with tints and reflectivity taken from real architectural glass
[*][b]Reflections of your city[/b]: the streets, cars and towers on screen, with the fog, the clouds and the lights
[*][b]The buildings around[/b]: the towers across the street show in the glass, even where the screen can't show them
[*][b]A live sky[/b] in the glass that follows the time of day and the weather, with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3805863507]Volumetric Weather[/url]'s clouds when you use it
[*][b]Panes that bend the reflections[/b] like real facades
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Works with any building asset, with no setup: what counts as glass is read from each asset's own textures.
Tested in my own game next to my usual mods, among them [b]Volumetric Weather[/b], [b]Render It![/b], [b]AD Cloud Replacer[/b], [b]Move It![/b], [b]TM:PE[/b] and [b]FPS Booster[/b], with no conflicts found.
Made on Windows. Mac and Linux versions are included (Linux is not tested, please share feedback).
[b]Removing the mod[/b] is safe: it saves nothing in your cities.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]For asset creators[/h1]
You don't need to do anything special, the mod reads the textures your asset already has:
[list]
[*][b]Specular map ([i]_s[/i])[/b]: anything 70% or brighter (180 out of 255) counts as glass, and no specular map means no glass. Keep shiny things that aren't glass (like metal) below 70%, or they turn into glass too.
[*][b]Window frames[/b]: paint them darker in the specular map (below 60%) and the panes will follow your windows up close.
[*][b]LODs[/b]: if you make your own LOD textures, include a specular map ([i]_lod_s[/i]) too, or the glass disappears when you zoom out.
[/list]
To check your building, open it in the [b]Asset Editor[/b]: the glass is there too, and zooming out shows your LOD's glass. Turn on [i]Show what counts as glass[/i] (Glass tab) and the glass turns magenta.
The glass pauses while you use the Snapshot tool, so your asset's thumbnails and snapshots show it the way everyone else sees it.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]RealGlass.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Real Glass) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
