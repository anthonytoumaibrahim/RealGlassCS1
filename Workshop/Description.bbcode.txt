Breathe life into your city's skyscrapers -- especially the ugly vanilla ones.
Adds a glass shader on all buildings with [b]screen space reflections (SSR)[/b] of the city around them and a live sky (reflecting the clouds in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3805863507]Volumetric Weather[/url]).
Tested only on my Windows 11 PC and Macbook. If anything looks wrong or runs badly, let me know in the comments.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and then this mod, make sure both are enabled. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a savegame[/b] (or the editors).
[*][b]Press F8[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the in-game panel, or press [b]Alt+G[/b] to customize an individual building.
[/olist]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]The panel (F8)[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Glass[/b]: turn the glass on or off, pick a real kind of glass (or your own color, reflectivity and clarity), and choose what counts as glass.
[*][b]Reflections[/b]: what the glass mirrors -- the city on screen, the buildings around, fog and clouds, the live sky -- plus sun glint and smoothness.
[*][b]Panes[/b]: pane size and story height, and how the panes bend reflections (cushioned panes, tilt, ripple).
[*][b]Water[/b] (off by default): the water's brightness, and the sky and city reflected in it (blur, streaks, reflection length).
[/list]
[b]Alt+G[/b] picks a building and opens a small window for its asset's own glass, on every copy in every city.
The mod's options page (Options → Real Glass) holds the keys and the [b]Rendering[/b] settings: how far the glass reaches (near, middle or the whole city) and the live sky's detail, to trade looks for frame rate.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Glass[/b]: customizable color and reflection intensity. You can also retain a building's details under the glass, or reduce the glass opacity.
[*][b]Per-building customization[/b] (press Alt+G): useful in instances where the asset textures
[*][b]Better Water[/b] (press Alt+G): useful in instances where the asset textures
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Works with (most) vanilla and custom building asset. No conflicts found with Render It!, AD Cloud Replacer, Move It!, TM:PE, FPS Booster and more. Safe to remove as it saves nothing in your cities.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]For asset creators[/h1]
Anything 70% (default value) or brighter in your specular map is glass, so keep shiny metal below that, and give your own LOD textures a specular too. Check it in the Asset Editor with [i]Show what counts as glass[/i] (Glass tab).

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Known issues / limitations[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Glass flickering[/b]: this happens due to using TAA.
[*][b]Reflections are not accurate:[/b] this uses SSR, ray tracing is not supported by the game to provide accurate reflections.
[/list]

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Real Glass), make the problem happen again, then post [i]RealGlass.log[/i] (in [i]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/i]), the game's [i]output_log.txt[/i] and a screenshot in the comments. Both logs reset at every launch.
