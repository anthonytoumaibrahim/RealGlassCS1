Makes the glass on your buildings, especially skyscrapers, look real, with [b]screen space reflections[/b] of the city around them and a live sky.
Tested only on my Windows 11 PC and MacBook. If anything looks wrong or runs badly, let me know in the comments.

[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] ([b]required[/b])
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2966990700]Unified UI[/url] (optional)
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]How to use[/h1]
Load a city (or the Asset or Scenario Editor) and press [b]F8[/b] for every setting. The defaults are tested with LUTs and [b]Render It![/b]; [i]Options → Real Glass[/i] has the key and [i]Reset all settings to defaults[/i].

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Glass[/b]: ten kinds of real architectural glass or your own color, and what counts as glass
[*][b]Reflections[/b]: the city on screen, the buildings around, a live sky with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3805863507]Volumetric Weather[/url]'s clouds, the sun's glint
[*][b]Panes[/b]: their size, and how they bend the reflections like real facades
[*][b]Rendering[/b]: how far the glass is drawn, and the sky's detail
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Works with any building asset, no setup. No conflicts found with Volumetric Weather, Render It!, AD Cloud Replacer, Move It!, TM:PE or FPS Booster. Mac and Linux versions included (Linux untested). Safe to remove as it saves nothing in your cities.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]For asset creators[/h1]
Anything 70% or brighter in your specular map ([i]_s[/i]) is glass, so keep shiny metal below that, and give your own LOD textures an [i]_lod_s[/i] too. Check it in the Asset Editor with [i]Show what counts as glass[/i] (Glass tab).

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Real Glass), make the problem happen again, then post [i]RealGlass.log[/i] (in [i]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/i]), the game's [i]output_log.txt[/i] and a screenshot in the comments. Both logs reset at every launch.
