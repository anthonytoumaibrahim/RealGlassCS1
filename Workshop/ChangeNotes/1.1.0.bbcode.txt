[h1]Version 1.1.0[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]LODs show the model's glass[/b]: a building whose distant model has no glass, or has it in the wrong places, no longer loses its glass and its rooms as you zoom out: it takes the full model's glass by the building's shape. On by default (Glass tab); per building with Alt+G, [i]Glass on its LOD[/i].
[*][b]Dynamic waves[/b]: the sea follows the game's weather. Rain and storms raise the wind up to 15 m/s, the waves grow longer and rougher over a few game hours and calm down slowly after. On by default (Water tab); turn it off to set [i]Wind speed[/i] yourself, as before.
[*][b]Procedural Objects' buildings[/b]: buildings converted with Procedural Objects get the glass and the rooms too, reshaped, stacked or turned, and Alt+G picks them.
[*][b]Moon glitter[/b] and [b]glitter sizes[/b]: the moon's path of light on the water has its own strength, and each glitter a size (smaller is narrower, not dimmer).
[*][b]Folding headings[/b] and [b]tooltips[/b] in the panel: click a heading to fold its settings away or open them; the panel remembers which are open. Hover over a setting whose name leaves a question.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]The rooms behind the windows no longer fade into a flat glow when a building turns its lights on at night; the painted frames stay dark.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]The panel's settings sit under new headings in a new order; no setting changed, and your settings file keeps every name.
[*][i]Water controls[/i] is now [i]Realistic water[/i].
[*]The sun's glitter is half as wide by default (both sizes 50%); [i]Moon glitter[/i] starts at 35%.
[*]Your settings, building settings and saved cities keep their values; the new rows start at their defaults.
[/list]
