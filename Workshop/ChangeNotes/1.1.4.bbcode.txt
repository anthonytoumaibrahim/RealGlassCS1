[h1]Version 1.1.4[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Glass on vehicles[/b]: real glass on every vehicle's windows, with the street, the buildings and the sky in them, lenses on the lamps, and tinted windows on a share of cars (5% by default). On by default, on the new Vehicles tab.
[*][b]Clear coat on vehicle paint[/b]: the paint mirrors the sky and the street and catches the sun, as a car's lacquer does. Vehicles painted in a fixed livery don't get it yet.
[*][b]For asset creators[/b]: in the Asset Editor, F8 (or the mod's button) opens a window to set the glass of the building being made. It is saved beside the asset and uploaded with it; players with the mod get it in every city, and their own settings for the asset (Alt+G) still come first.
[*][b]Rooms lit by the asset's illumination map[/b]: the rooms light where and when the game lights the asset's windows. Off by default (Interiors tab, [i]Follow each asset's illumination map[/i]); per building with Alt+G too.
[*][b]Curtains per building[/b]: Alt+G has a [i]Curtains or blinds[/i] switch, for glass that is no room's window, such as a fire station's doors.
[*][b]Opacity by day[/b] for the rooms (Interiors tab, under [i]Show the rooms by day[/i]).
[*][b]Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese[/b]: shown with the game's language, or picked with [i]Show this mod in[/i] on the options page. Made with AI, so corrections are welcome.
[*][b]Foam in layers along the shores[/b]: a surf band some 70 m wide, waves breaking in sets with lace and bubbles behind them, and short white streaks out beyond the surf. Before, the game's steep coasts had one thin line of foam.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]Tall waves, as in a tsunami, are no longer see-through.
[*]Foam no longer speckles the open sea, jumping to new places every second.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]In the Asset Editor the creator window replaces the F8 panel; the building picker and the water stay out of it.
[*]Other mods come first: where this mod hooks the game's drawing of vehicles and water, other mods' changes are made first, whatever order the mods load in.
[*]A part that stops after an error (the glass on buildings, on vehicles, or the water) now says so in a message, once per session.
[*]With two different copies of the mod enabled (the Workshop's and one in Addons\Mods), the second one switches itself off and says so.
[*]Your settings, building settings and saved cities keep their values; the new settings start at their defaults.
[/list]
